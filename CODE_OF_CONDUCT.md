# Community code of conduct

CShrimpSharp aims to maintain a focused, respectful, and technically useful community.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss code and ideas without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect different experience levels and communication styles;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and deliberate disruption;
- protect private and security-sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. Serious concerns can be raised privately through the repository's security advisory channel.
